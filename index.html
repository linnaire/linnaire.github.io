01 — About

A developer who reads the business first, then the code.

I started out in 2014 debugging banking and membership systems, and spent those early years moving between whatever the job needed — VB.NET, Laravel, WordPress, .NET Core. That range turned out to be the useful part. ERP work is rarely one clean stack; it's a legacy system, a spreadsheet, an API that half-works, and a deadline.

Since 2020 my focus has been the Acumatica Framework. I've built core modules for real estate and property management from reservation through move-out, integrated in-house software with Acumatica SaaS over REST, customised the mobile app, shipped a ConnectWise integration, and built an automated installer for clients importing very large record sets. I'm also the person my team sends the upgrades to.

Alongside that I've freelanced on Upwork since 2015 — smaller, sharper projects where I own the whole thing end to end. Four times certified as an Acumatica Application Developer, most recently in 2026.
